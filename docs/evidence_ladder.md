# Evidence Ladder

This ladder keeps the computational path explicit.

| Level | Evidence available | Claim allowed | Next requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L0 | Physical model or literature hypothesis | Mechanistic hypothesis | Public-data test |
| L1 | Metadata or public molecular association | Exploratory association | Sample-level replication |
| L2 | Independent cohort replication | Reproducible biological signal | Cohort-specific calibration |
| L3 | External cohort with same endpoint/treatment | Transportable research score | Prospective clinical design |
| L4 | Prospective clinical validation | Clinical utility estimate | Implementation and monitoring |

## Current project position

  context, with cohort-specific endpoints.
- No current result is L3 or L4.

## Claim discipline

Every manuscript claim should identify its evidence level, unit of replication,
endpoint, treatment context, and unresolved confounders. A result cannot advance
to the next level merely by adding more cells; it needs compatible patients,
endpoints, and independent data.
